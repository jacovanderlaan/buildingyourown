Topic

Data, rules and structure first

Most AI tools fail on the data, not on the AI. Clean data, written rules and one place for every fact are what make a tool work.

  • A household and business ledger you own
  • Every booking linked to its scan
  • From 95 mails to the one that needs you
  • One source, three publications: birthdays
  • Every receipt filed on the day it happened
  • From phone scan to the right folder, named and indexed
  • Your mail archive as a database you own
