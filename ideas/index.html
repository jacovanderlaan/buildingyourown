Ideas we are exploring

Open ideas for the Build Days. Some will happen, some will not.

An alumni group of buildersA small chat group per Build Day; after three groups, one shared group. Outcome figures after 30 and 90 days.
Build Days abroad with a local hostAttach a Build Day to a trip that happens anyway; a coworking space or chamber of commerce brings the room and the group.
The EU digital maturity scan as pre-workParticipants do the free EU DMAT before the day, and again at the return session: a standard before-and-after measure.
A guest builder on every Build DayOne SME owner who already built something tells the story in fifteen minutes.
A Build Day hired by an innovation hubThe hub pays, the SME attends for free, the room is full. The build step after a hub's free ideation workshop.
A one-page use case and data canvasThe output of the questionnaire and the call: problem, business case, data quality, privacy — on one page. Also a marketing asset.
One use case per month as an articleEach use case on this site becomes a LinkedIn post and an article, with the real numbers.
