First Time Right

Do it properly the first time; the shortcut you skip now is the rework you pay for later, with interest.

Jaco van der Laan

The idea

There is a persistent illusion that speed and quality trade off. Over any real horizon the opposite holds: the corner you cut moves downstream and grows. A half-done job becomes a bug, a bug becomes an incident, an incident becomes a rebuild. First time right is paying the price up front, when it is smallest. It is not perfectionism; it is not leaving known weakness behind you.

On a Build Day

The counterweight to fast vibe coding. Small and quick is fine, but the one rule your tool depends on gets a test before you rely on it.
