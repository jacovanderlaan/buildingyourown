Anthropic Academy

Free courses on working with Claude, from prompting to building agents.

Anthropic

What it is

Anthropic Academy is Anthropic's free learning platform: short courses on using Claude well, from the basics of prompting to Claude Code, the API and building agents with the Model Context Protocol.

How I use it

I keep learning at the source. The courses are where I check my own habits against how the makers of the model intend it to be used, and they are a good starting point to recommend before a Build Day.

On a Build Day

Not required. If you have never used Claude, one introductory course beforehand helps you spend the day on your own problem instead of on the interface.

Watch out

A course teaches the tool. The day teaches the structure around it: your data, your rules and a way to check the result.

Concepts behind it

  • Stop Prompting, Start Directing — Set up a system before the task; give AI an outcome, context, constraints and a way to validate.
  • Stay Sharp with AI — The common fear is that AI makes us dumber. The answer isn't to avoid AI — it's to stay the curator and decision-maker, keep thinking, and keep learning, so AI amplifies your judgment instead of replacing it.
  • Vibe Coding — Use AI to quickly build real things — especially nice interfaces on top of the metadata — to explore, prototype and give people a way in. Great for experimenting; just don't ship the unspecified, ungated style to production.
  • Write the Spec, Not the Prompt — A prompt is a request you repeat. A spec is a contract the work is checked against — and it is the artefact that survives the session.
