Jaco van der Laan

The rule

One of the rules I live by: build something you did not have to. Then use it, fix it, and use it again. The building is not the point; staying in the habit of making something that works is.

On a Build Day

This is the builder's mindset the day is meant to start. The first tool does not have to be important. It has to work, and you have to use it, so the second one comes easier.

Concepts behind it

  • Play with the Blocks — Like Lego — snap, try, rebuild. Tinker in your own life to learn what's possible.
  • Eat Your Own Dogfood — Use your own tool/method on your own real work before and while you ask anyone else to — dogfooding surfaces the flaws a demo hides.
  • Stay Sharp with AI — The common fear is that AI makes us dumber. The answer isn't to avoid AI — it's to stay the curator and decision-maker, keep thinking, and keep learning, so AI amplifies your judgment instead of replacing it.
