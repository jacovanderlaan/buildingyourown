Zettelkasten

One idea per note, each note linked to its neighbours. The structure is what the links turn out to be.

Niklas Luhmann

The idea

Niklas Luhmann's note system: one idea per note, each note linked to its neighbours, and no master hierarchy decided up front.

What I took from it is the atomic unit. A note that says one thing can be reused; a note that says six things can only be read.

On a Build Day

AI works best on small, separate, well-named pieces of information: one record per customer, one file per document, one rule per line. The same atomic unit makes your data usable.
