Design and small experiments · book

Designing Your Life

Design thinking applied to your own life: prototype conversations and small trials before big decisions. It translates directly to a company's first AI tool.

Bill Burnett & Dave Evans · 2016

What the book is about

Bill Burnett and Dave Evans teach the Stanford design-thinking course and apply it to careers and life choices: empathise, define, ideate, prototype, test. Instead of "finding your passion", you design and try.

Ideas I took from it

  • A method, not a revelation. A repeatable design process replaces waiting for the one right answer.
  • Sketch several futures in parallel. Three genuinely different plans stop you from committing too early.
  • Prototype before you migrate. Small conversations and trials de-risk a direction faster than analysis.

On a Build Day

The same move works for a company's first AI tool: build a small prototype on real data, try it, and only then decide whether it deserves more.

Concepts behind it

  • Design Thinking — Approach modeling as design: understand the real need, explore options, and experiment fast — and with AI you can now try new methods, build applications and test them at a speed that was impossible before.
  • The Experiment Loop — Play with a hypothesis: hypothesize → experiment → learn → decide. The mechanism that turns tinkering into a proven block.
  • Fail Fast — Set experiments up to fail quickly and cheaply, read the signal honestly, and move on — don't linger in something that, in the end, may not work.
