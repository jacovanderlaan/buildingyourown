Resources

Favourite resources

Books from my own shelf, and the tools and courses the use cases on this site were built with.

Books

Building a Second BrainTiago Forte · 2022 Capture, organise, distil, express. A personal knowledge system is the smallest version of a company brain.
Clean ArchitectureRobert C. Martin · 2017 Keep the rules of the business apart from the tools around them. That separation is what lets you swap the AI, the database or the screen later.
The Data Model Resource Book (Universal Data Models)Len Silverston Proven patterns for parties, agreements, products and work efforts. The CRM replacement on this site uses its Work Effort pattern.
Designing Your LifeBill Burnett & Dave Evans · 2016 Design thinking applied to your own life: prototype conversations and small trials before big decisions. It translates directly to a company's first AI tool.
Do DesignAlan Moore · 2016 A short book about design as a way of working, not a department.
Extreme OwnershipJocko Willink & Leif Babin · 2015 If you build it, you own it — including the import that silently stopped running.
Getting Things DoneDavid Allen · 2001 The original inbox discipline: everything gets captured, clarified and given one next action. Email triage starts here.
The Great Mental Models, Volume 1Shane Parrish & Rhiannon Beaubien · 2019 First principles and inversion: ask what would make the tool fail, and build that check first.
The Pragmatic Programmer (20th anniversary edition)David Thomas & Andrew Hunt · 2019 Tracer bullets, small steps, don't repeat yourself. Written for programmers, and more relevant now that the AI is the one typing.
Semantic Software DesignEben Hewitt · 2019 Design starts with meaning: name the concepts of the business before choosing any technology.
Super ThinkingGabriel Weinberg & Lauren McCann · 2019 A catalogue of mental models; good for naming what you are doing when you scope a problem.
Thinking in Systems: A PrimerDonella H. Meadows · 2008 Stocks, flows and feedback loops. Useful for seeing why a tool that works on day one can drift by month three.
Tiny ExperimentsAnne-Laure Le Cunff · 2025 Treat a change as an experiment with a fixed duration instead of a goal. The same move is what makes a Build Day work: one small build, then decide.
Visual ThinkingWillemien Brand · 2017 Drawing the problem before building it. Most scoping on a Build Day happens on paper first.

Tools

Claude CodeAnthropic The AI coding tool most of the use cases on this site were built with. Strong when it gets a spec, rules and a way to check its own work.
DuckDBDuckDB Foundation A database in a single file, no server. Ideal as a rebuildable mirror of files you own.
