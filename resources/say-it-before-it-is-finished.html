The rule

Say it out loud before it is finished. Half of it is wrong, and there is no other way to find out. Waiting for it to be right means finding out later, when it costs more.

On a Build Day

At the end of the day everyone demos what they built, finished or not. The feedback on a half-working tool is worth more than a polished one nobody has seen.

  • On Breakthrough Sabbatical

Concepts behind it

  • Fail Fast — Set experiments up to fail quickly and cheaply, read the signal honestly, and move on — don't linger in something that, in the end, may not work.
  • Reality Check — Test the model and the plan against the real world — the actual data, deliveries and behavior — not against the assumption you started with.
  • Proof, Not Slides — Ground every claim in real numbers and real systems; never fabricate.
