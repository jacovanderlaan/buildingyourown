Building a Second Brain

Capture, organise, distil, express. A personal knowledge system is the smallest version of a company brain.

Tiago Forte · 2022

What the book is about

Tiago Forte's method for a personal knowledge system. Four steps, CODE: capture what resonates, organise it, distil it to the essence, and express it in your own work. PARA sorts everything by how actionable it is: projects, areas, resources and archives.

Ideas I took from it

  • Organise by use, not by subject. A note goes where you will need it next, not where it belongs in a library.
  • Distil in layers. Every time you touch a note, make it a little easier to read the next time.
  • A system is for output. Notes that never turn into work are a hobby, not a system.

On a Build Day

A company brain starts the same way: one folder convention and one place for every document, before any AI is added.
