Mental models, rules and habits · model

Compounding

Small consistent returns beat large occasional ones, given time. What matters is the number of deposits, not the size of one.

Jaco van der Laan

The idea

The financial idea applied to everything with a time axis: knowledge, structure, relationships, health. What matters is not the size of one deposit but the number of them and how long they are left alone.

The uncomfortable part is that the early curve looks flat, which is exactly when people stop.

On a Build Day

Many small tools that each save an hour a week beat one big AI project that takes a year. Every block you build makes the next one faster, because you reuse it.

Concepts behind it

  • Compounding Efficiency — Spend time, money and attention where they compound — cut the meetings, the licensed bloat and the unstructured chat that add nothing, and put the resource into structure that pays back.
  • Play Personally → Prove the Block → Apply in Business — The flow from personal experiment to business application.
  • Reusable Skills — Reusable, AI-readable units of behavior — a skill packages how to do one thing well, versioned and callable, so people and AI apply it the same way every time.
