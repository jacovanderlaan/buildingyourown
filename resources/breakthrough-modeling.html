Breakthrough Modeling

Data modeling for the AI era — including the case for building your own modeling app instead of paying for a tool you use a fraction of.

Jaco van der Laan

What it is

Breakthrough Modeling is my method for data modeling now that AI writes much of the code. It starts from proven industry patterns instead of a blank page, keeps the model as metadata, and generates the rest from it.

Why it matters for building your own

Most home-made tools break on the data, not on the code. A good model, taken from a pattern that many companies already proved, is the cheapest way to make a small tool last. The CRM on this site, for example, follows Len Silverston's Work Effort pattern.

On a Build Day

You do not need to know any modeling. We take the part of a proven pattern your use case needs, and leave the rest.

Concepts behind it

  • Proven Patterns — Proven shapes you reach for instead of inventing — a pattern captures a repeatable solution (Data Vault, dimensional, temporal) so you compose from known-good parts.
  • Do Not Reinvent the Wheel — The new way of modeling stands on the shoulders of giants — distil the essentials from decades of data-modeling work and keep what works, rather than starting from scratch.
  • Metadata First — Metadata is the material Breakthrough Modeling is built from — structure, logic and meaning captured as data, so the platform and the AI can both read and run it.
  • Model the Process, Not Just the Model — Model not just the data model and the transformations, but the whole process — delivery, integration, migration, quality checking, the backroom — and how the system changes over time.
  • Decompose and Compose — Break the complex whole into small, self-contained parts you can name, model and check one at a time — the core move of thinking in structures.
