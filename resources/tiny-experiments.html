Design and small experiments · book

Tiny Experiments

Treat a change as an experiment with a fixed duration instead of a goal. The same move is what makes a Build Day work: one small build, then decide.

Anne-Laure Le Cunff · 2025

What the book is about

Anne-Laure Le Cunff (Ness Labs) replaces fixed goals with tiny experiments: a commitment with a set duration, followed by an honest review. Curiosity guides the next step, not a borrowed plan.

Ideas I took from it

  • A pact instead of a goal. Commit to a process for a fixed period, then look at what it gave you.
  • Plus, minus, next. A light review keeps the learning going without turning it into a project.
  • Follow the signal of your own experiments.

On a Build Day

A Build Day is one tiny experiment: one small build on your own data, then a clear decision to keep it, change it or drop it.

Links

  • Ness Labs

Concepts behind it

  • The Experiment Loop — Play with a hypothesis: hypothesize → experiment → learn → decide. The mechanism that turns tinkering into a proven block.
  • Fail Fast — Set experiments up to fail quickly and cheaply, read the signal honestly, and move on — don't linger in something that, in the end, may not work.
  • Play with the Blocks — Like Lego — snap, try, rebuild. Tinker in your own life to learn what's possible.
