Dawarich

Self-hosted location history, an alternative to Google Timeline. Where you were becomes data you own.

Dawarich

What it is

Dawarich is an open-source app for your own location history: it records where you have been from your phone, imports an old Google Timeline export, and shows trips, visits and statistics on a map, on your own server.

How I use it

It runs on my home server next to the photo library and the document system. Location is one of the threads of my calendar: it helps to date a scan, place a photo or reconstruct a trip.

On a Build Day

For a business, location data is often hidden in travel claims, site visits or delivery routes. Owning it yourself turns it into something you can query, instead of something a vendor keeps.

Watch out

Location history is personal data of the most sensitive kind. Keep it on your own hardware, back it up, and decide up front who may see it.
