Design and small experiments · book

Do Design

A short book about design as a way of working, not a department.

Alan Moore · 2016

What the book is about

Alan Moore's short book in the Do series argues that design is not a department or a finishing touch, but a way of making things with care: paying attention to the people who use them, and to how they feel.

Ideas I took from it

  • Design is how you work, not what you add at the end.
  • Care shows. People notice when something was made with attention, also in a small internal tool.
  • Simple takes effort. A clear result is usually the outcome of leaving things out.

On a Build Day

A tool you build for your own team only gets used if it is pleasant to use. We spend part of the day on that, not only on whether it works.

Concepts behind it

  • Design Thinking — Approach modeling as design: understand the real need, explore options, and experiment fast — and with AI you can now try new methods, build applications and test them at a speed that was impossible before.
  • Deliberate Simplicity — Make each part as plain as it can be without losing what matters — reuse only the fraction you actually need, and strip the rest.
