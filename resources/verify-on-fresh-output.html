Verify on fresh output

Verify on fresh output, not on what the script printed. Check the artefact, not the log line claiming it is there.

Jaco van der Laan

The habit

A build once printed "copied 80" while all 80 images had silently vanished: a file name had been rewritten and the check looked at the old one. The report was true and useless.

So the check is on the artefact: does the file exist, does the page render, does the link work. Not on the log line claiming it does.

On a Build Day

When AI writes the code, this is the most important habit of all. The AI will tell you it is done. You open the result and look.

Concepts behind it

  • Never Marks Its Own Homework — The judge must be separate from the builder, and the score is the off-switch: a loop only stops when an independent evaluator says the rubric is met.
  • A Confident Error Has No Tell — A hallucination arrives in exactly the same shape as a correct answer. That is the whole problem — and why fluency is not evidence.
  • Uptime Is Not Correctness — Monitoring proves the system ran. Observability for AI has to prove the answer was right — and those are different instruments.
