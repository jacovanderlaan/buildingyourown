Data, rules and structure first

Most AI tools fail on the data, not on the AI. Clean data, written rules and one place for every fact are what make a tool work. 8 concepts; each opens its full page in its own library.

Structure Beats MagicThe magic isn't in the model; it's in the structure you give it.Structure Beats Magic →Rules as DataConstraints and logic expressed as data, not buried in code — rules say what must hold, versioned and enforceable, so quality and governance travel with the model.Breakthrough Modeling →Rules You Can Verify'2-space indent' is a rule; 'format code properly' is a wish. A rule the system can't check is decoration.Structure Beats Magic →Deterministic by DesignDeliberate is whether the structure was chosen; deterministic is whether the output repeats. A toy is neither on the second try.Structure Beats Magic →Proven PatternsProven shapes you reach for instead of inventing — a pattern captures a repeatable solution (Data Vault, dimensional, temporal) so you compose from known-good parts.Breakthrough Modeling →Deliberate SimplicityMake each part as plain as it can be without losing what matters — reuse only the fraction you actually need, and strip the rest.Breakthrough Modeling →The Calendar Is the SpineOne time-axis is the backbone of a second brain — everything dated hangs off it.Structure Beats Magic →One-Way PublishingYou don't sync systems — you publish from one.
