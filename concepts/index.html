The ideas behind it

63 concepts from the Structure Beats Magic and Breakthrough Modeling libraries that matter most when you build your own tools. Each one opens its full page in its own library.

Design thinking and small experiments

Design ThinkingApproach modeling as design: understand the real need, explore options, and experiment fast — and with AI you can now try new methods, build applications and test them at a speed that was impossible before.Breakthrough Modeling →Fail FastSet experiments up to fail quickly and cheaply, read the signal honestly, and move on — don't linger in something that, in the end, may not work.Breakthrough Modeling →The Experiment LoopPlay with a hypothesis: hypothesize → experiment → learn → decide. The mechanism that turns tinkering into a proven block.Structure Beats Magic →Play with the BlocksLike Lego — snap, try, rebuild. Tinker in your own life to learn what's possible.Structure Beats Magic →Play Personally → Prove the Block → Apply in BusinessThe flow from personal experiment to business application.Structure Beats Magic →Find the Essence FirstDistil the essence before the detail — in interviews, grasp the data and the bigger picture first, then drill down; and quickly identify what actually works in the giants who came before us.Breakthrough Modeling →Decompose and ComposeBreak the complex whole into small, self-contained parts you can name, model and check one at a time — the core move of thinking in structures.Breakthrough Modeling →

From proof of concept to something you rely on

Proof, Not SlidesGround every claim in real numbers and real systems; never fabricate.Structure Beats Magic →A Toy vs an InstrumentWithout structure AI is a toy; with structure it becomes an instrument.Structure Beats Magic →The Demo Agent and the Production AgentThe demo is one prompt, one tool call, one context window. Production is planning, layered memory, guardrails, observability and eval loops — and the gap is not model quality.Structure Beats Magic →Reality CheckTest the model and the plan against the real world — the actual data, deliveries and behavior — not against the assumption you started with.Breakthrough Modeling →Eat Your Own DogfoodUse your own tool/method on your own real work before and while you ask anyone else to — dogfooding surfaces the flaws a demo hides.Structure Beats Magic →

Vibe coding practices that hold

Vibe CodingUse AI to quickly build real things — especially nice interfaces on top of the metadata — to explore, prototype and give people a way in. Great for experimenting; just don't ship the unspecified, ungated style to production.Breakthrough Modeling →Write the Spec, Not the PromptA prompt is a request you repeat. A spec is a contract the work is checked against — and it is the artefact that survives the session.Structure Beats Magic →Stop Prompting, Start DirectingSet up a system before the task; give AI an outcome, context, constraints and a way to validate.Structure Beats Magic →CLAUDE.md as Operating SystemStanding rules the AI reads before it acts — a layered OS for how the assistant behaves in your world.Structure Beats Magic →Hooks Over HopesEvery instruction to a model is a probability, not a promise. Rules that must always run belong in deterministic automation the model can't skip.Structure Beats Magic →Evals Are the Release GateAn eval suite that doesn't block a release isn't a gate, it's a report. No evals, no ship.Structure Beats Magic →The Model Proposes, the Harness DisposesGive the model no direct external actions. It reasons and suggests; the surrounding layer validates, executes and records — that split is what makes an agent governable.Structure Beats Magic →Never Marks Its Own HomeworkThe judge must be separate from the builder, and the score is the off-switch: a loop only stops when an independent evaluator says the rubric is met.Structure Beats Magic →The Mistakes FileSame mistake twice = a new rule. Corrections you'd otherwise repeat forever get written into a file the AI reads every run.Structure Beats Magic →A Confident Error Has No TellA hallucination arrives in exactly the same shape as a correct answer. That is the whole problem — and why fluency is not evidence.Structure Beats Magic →Reusable SkillsReusable, AI-readable units of behavior — a skill packages how to do one thing well, versioned and callable, so people and AI apply it the same way every time.Breakthrough Modeling →Stay Sharp with AIThe common fear is that AI makes us dumber. The answer isn't to avoid AI — it's to stay the curator and decision-maker, keep thinking, and keep learning, so AI amplifies your judgment instead of replacing it.Breakthrough Modeling →

Data, rules and structure first

Structure Beats MagicThe magic isn't in the model; it's in the structure you give it.Structure Beats Magic →Rules as DataConstraints and logic expressed as data, not buried in code — rules say what must hold, versioned and enforceable, so quality and governance travel with the model.Breakthrough Modeling →Rules You Can Verify'2-space indent' is a rule; 'format code properly' is a wish. A rule the system can't check is decoration.Structure Beats Magic →Deterministic by DesignDeliberate is whether the structure was chosen; deterministic is whether the output repeats. A toy is neither on the second try.Structure Beats Magic →Proven PatternsProven shapes you reach for instead of inventing — a pattern captures a repeatable solution (Data Vault, dimensional, temporal) so you compose from known-good parts.Breakthrough Modeling →Deliberate SimplicityMake each part as plain as it can be without losing what matters — reuse only the fraction you actually need, and strip the rest.Breakthrough Modeling →The Calendar Is the SpineOne time-axis is the backbone of a second brain — everything dated hangs off it.Structure Beats Magic →One-Way PublishingYou don't sync systems — you publish from one. Sync is where rot starts.Structure Beats Magic →

Build, buy or rent

Own the Layer or Rent the FeatureEvery knowledge product answers two questions: who maintains it, and is it a layer of its own or a feature of something else. The answers decide what you can build on.Structure Beats Magic →Rent the AI; Own the StructureModels are swappable commodities; your structure is the durable asset.Structure Beats Magic →The Model Is the Easy PartEveryone can rent the same model. What surrounds it — context, rules, memory, verification — is the only part that can be yours.Structure Beats Magic →Bankers vs BuildersThe discipline of governance meeting the leverage of builders — speak both.Structure Beats Magic →Do Not Reinvent the WheelThe new way of modeling stands on the shoulders of giants — distil the essentials from decades of data-modeling work and keep what works, rather than starting from scratch.Breakthrough Modeling →

Privacy and control

Silence Is Not Absence of FailureNo complaints does not mean no problems. It usually means people adapted to the errors and stopped mentioning them.Structure Beats Magic →Uptime Is Not CorrectnessMonitoring proves the system ran. Observability for AI has to prove the answer was right — and those are different instruments.Structure Beats Magic →Data ObservabilityContinuous visibility into the health of your data pipelines — freshness, volume, schema drift, distribution and quality — so problems surface on their own, at the boundary, in time to fix them.Breakthrough Modeling →Facts ExpireKnowledge doesn't become false, it becomes superseded. A store that only accumulates is a store that quietly gets less true.Structure Beats Magic →Guardrails Are LayersA guardrail is not one filter at the end. It's seven checkpoints with different jobs — and the one everyone skips is memory.Structure Beats Magic →Incident ModelingModel delivery incidents as data, not loose tickets — a late feed, a failed quality check, a broken schema becomes a tracked, owned, queryable object in the model itself.Breakthrough Modeling →Escalate by ExceptionRoute the easy work to the cheap path and reserve the expensive one for what actually needs it. Power by exception, not by default.Structure Beats Magic →
