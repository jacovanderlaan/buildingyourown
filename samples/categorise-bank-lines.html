Categorise bank lines with a rule file

One month of anonymised bank lines in, a categorised overview and a rule file out.

Based on: A household and business ledger you own · Level: beginner · Time: 45 minutes live, or one Build Day afternoon

Steps

  1. Export one month of bank lines; replace names with made-up ones.
  2. Write five rules you already apply in your head: pattern → category.
  3. Ask the AI to build a small script that applies the rules and lists what no rule matched.
  4. Add rules until the unmatched list is short. That list is your real work.
  5. Show the overview per category.

What you take home

A working result on example data, the rules written down, and the list of cases the rules did not cover — the honest next step.
