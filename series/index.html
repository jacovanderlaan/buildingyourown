The Build Your Own series

Build Your Own Brain

Fifteen things you can build for your own business, one level at a time, inside two frames that keep it safe and working.

Your business already has a brain: in your head, your mailbox and a dozen apps. This series shows how to build it yourself, so you and your AI can use it. Everyone wants the dashboard. It only works on the layers underneath: a memory that is ordered, data you can trust, rules the AI follows. This series names what you can build for your own business, in the order you build it.

What you can build now

An entrepreneur can now build what used to need a developer and a project plan, in a day. The AI does most of the typing. You decide what it must do, and you check that it does.

The proof is in my own system. The CRM I rent is being replaced by a cockpit I built on my own copy of the data: 7,574 people, 4,106 companies and 992 projects, with the screens generated by AI (Replacing a CRM before the renewal date). And when that copy turned out to have stopped updating 20 months earlier, finding the cause, reducing three import scripts to one and adding a check that fails loudly after seven days took one day (The mirror that went stale and nobody noticed).

One formula, five layers

The series follows one line: Structure + Data + AI + Rules → Intelligence. Each layer rests on the one before it.

Layer Build Your Own … The question it answers
Structure Memory · Models Where does everything live, and what is a thing?
Data Sources · Pipelines · Checks · Automations · Integrations What comes in, can you trust it, and does it keep flowing?
AI Skills Which recurring work does the AI do on your structure?
Rules Rules What must the AI and the people always, and never, do?
→ Intelligence Insights · Dashboards · Planner · Apps · Interfaces · Control Plane What do you get out of it?

Two frames run around all five layers: Safeguards asks is it safe?, Monitoring asks is it working?

Pick your day

One Build Day is one or a few levels, chosen in the intake call. Four examples:

  • Get organised — Memory + Models + Rules. You leave with your business in folders, a small data model, and the first rules your AI follows.
  • Your data, live — Sources + Pipelines + Checks. One export comes in by itself, gets cleaned, and a red/green list tells you what is wrong with it.
  • Your AI at work — Integrations + Skills + Automations. Your AI reads one system you use, does one recurring task, and runs it on a schedule.
  • See everything — Insights + Dashboards + Control Plane. Three questions answered from your own data, on one overview for you and one for your team.

Bring your own idea

You don't have to pick a level. Come with the problem that costs you the most time, and the day is built around it, with whichever levels it needs. The intake call finds out which foundation is missing.

Where to start

If your documents are scattered, that is Memory. If your numbers don't add up, that is Checks. Start where your foundation is weakest.
