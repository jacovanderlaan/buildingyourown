Accounting and bank transactions

Bank lines, booking rules, receipts and the link between a scan and its booking, next to the accounting package you already have.

Keep the accounting package, own the booking rulesThe official books stay in the accounting package. The knowledge of how things are booked moves out of people's heads into a readable rule file, and a mirror of the books makes them queryable.A household and business ledger you ownBank exports from several banks and a credit card land in one database, every row tagged personal or business and categorised by readable rules. The finance app becomes a view, not a vault.Every booking linked to its scanScans carry date, vendor and amount in a small metadata file; bookings come from the accounting export. A match on date and amount links them, and two short lists show what is missing on either side.in progressEvery receipt filed on the day it happenedReceipts, invoices and statements are filed as PDFs in the folder of the date they belong to, each with a small metadata file next to it. Matching them to bank lines becomes a join on date and amount.
