Accounting and bank transactions

Every receipt filed on the day it happened

Receipts, invoices and statements are filed as PDFs in the folder of the date they belong to, each with a small metadata file next to it. Matching them to bank lines becomes a join on date and amount.

Replaces
a shoebox, a receipts app, a shared drive full of scans
Built with
folder convention, YAML sidecars, Python
In numbers
4,289 receipts in one cockpit

The situation

Receipts end up everywhere: e-mail, a phone scan, a drawer, a folder per supplier. At year end someone spends days matching them to the bank.

What I built

One rule: a document lives in the folder of the day it happened. Next to every PDF sits a small YAML file with type, vendor, category and amount. Statements are filed by the period they cover. Dossiers link to documents; they never copy them.

The structure underneath

  • Time is the spine. Everything dated has exactly one home.
  • The sidecar is the index. A script reads all sidecars into a table; the receipts cockpit is a view on that table.
  • Matching is a join, not a search: same date, same amount.
