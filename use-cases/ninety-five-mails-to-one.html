Email and inbox management

From 95 mails to the one that needs you

Existing mail filters and a set of decisions per sender become one classification, and a daily summary shows only what needs a human.

Replaces
inbox zero by hand
Built with
mail filters as data, sender classification, a daily note
In numbers
2,287 filters ingested · 1,380 sender decisions

The situation

Most mail is not for you personally: newsletters, notifications, receipts. The few mails that matter drown.

What I built

All existing mail filters were read in as data. I made one decision per frequent sender — about 1,380 of them. Every morning the day's mail is classified and the daily note shows a short triage section. On a typical day 95 mails became one that needed me.

The structure underneath

The decisions are the asset. Once written down, a script or an AI can apply them for years.

Concepts behind it

  • Inbox Triage — Every place loose input arrives needs a fast per-item sort — process, archive, discard, defer — before anything reaches the vault.
  • Email Intelligence — Classify every sender signal-vs-noise; turn an inbox into a managed source.
