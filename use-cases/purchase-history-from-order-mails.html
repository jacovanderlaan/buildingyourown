A purchase history from order confirmations

Every web shop sends an order confirmation. Read them all into one table and you have a purchase history across every shop — warranty dates included.

Replaces
logging in to every web shop to find an old order
Built with
mail export, extraction script, DuckDB
In numbers
2,904 order mails from one large web shop · 2,519 from one retail chain

The situation

Where did we buy that laptop, and is it still under warranty? The answer is in a confirmation mail from three years ago, in a web shop account nobody remembers the password for.

What I am building

A step that finds order confirmations in the mail archive, pulls out shop, date, items and amount, and writes them into one purchase table. The design is decided; extraction scripts exist and were tried on the largest senders.

Status

In progress. Thousands of order mails are identified per shop; the full extraction and the screen are not built yet. It is on this site because the design choices — one table, every shop, the mail as source — are the interesting part.

The structure underneath

A purchase links to a product, a supplier, a warranty and a booking. Once it is a row, all four become questions you can answer.

Start on a Build Day

Search your mail for one shop's order confirmations. Let the AI extract date, items and amount from ten of them, and check every one.

Concepts behind it

  • Fail Fast — Set experiments up to fail quickly and cheaply, read the signal honestly, and move on — don't linger in something that, in the end, may not work.
  • The Experiment Loop — Play with a hypothesis: hypothesize → experiment → learn → decide. The mechanism that turns tinkering into a proven block.
