Privacy

Privacy statement

This statement covers buildingyourown.ai and the registration for Build Days. It is short on purpose.

Who is responsible

Jaco van der Laan is responsible for the personal data collected through this site (the "controller" under the GDPR). Questions or requests: send a message via LinkedIn.

What is collected, and why

When you register, you give your name, contact details, company details and your answers about your use case, your data and your experience. They are used for one purpose: to prepare your Build Day — the 20-minute call, the group composition, the starter kit and the day itself.

If you tick the publication box, your case may be described anonymously in an article. You are always asked again, about that specific text, before anything is published.

What is not collected

The site sets no tracking cookies and uses no analytics. Do not put customer data, passwords or confidential documents in the form; describe them instead.

Where it is stored

Your answers are sent through form.taxi, a form service in the EU, which delivers them to Jaco by email. After that they are kept privately by Jaco. They are not shared with other participants, sold or used for marketing lists.

How long

Answers and call notes are deleted twelve months after your Build Day, unless you ask to stay in contact. You can ask for deletion at any time before that.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, at any time. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
